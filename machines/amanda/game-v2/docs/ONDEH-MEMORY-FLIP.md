# Ondeh-Ondeh minigame — Memory Flip

## Story purpose

This is not a trivia test. Ondeh-Ondeh's sensory memories have separated into pairs. Matching related images helps reconnect what was outside, what was inside, and how the character was made.

## Six conceptual pairs

1. Pandan leaf ↔ fragrant green swirl.
2. Grated coconut ↔ coconut coating around the character.
3. Palm-sugar block ↔ warm liquid centre.
4. Soft dough ↔ rolled round ball.
5. Steamer ↔ rising steam.
6. Ondeh-Ondeh ↔ golden restored-memory sparkle.

The generated art sheet is a style prototype and repeats some subjects rather than delivering twelve perfectly distinct production tiles. Claude should use temporary atlas crops only. Final individual tile files should be generated after the first interaction test confirms the required dimensions.

## Interaction

- Start with cards face down on the tray.
- Reveal two at a time.
- A conceptual pair remains face up and reveals one short memory line.
- A mismatch turns back after a gentle pause.
- No timer, score penalty, or move limit in story mode.
- Allow touch, click, and keyboard selection.
- Use a 4x3 grid on desktop and 3x4 or 2x4 reduced set on narrow/short phones.
- Never require matching by color alone.

## Emotional progression

Early matches recall vague sensations. The centre/filling match should occur late if possible, but do not rig the board so heavily that replay feels dishonest. The final match creates the warm golden glow used in Ondeh-Ondeh's remembering expression.

Suggested fictional memory lines:

- “Something green… soft, not sharp. It smelled like a garden after rain.”
- “These little white pieces were all around me. They made me feel dressed for somewhere special.”
- “There it is—the warmth I was frightened of. It wasn't hurting me. It was waiting inside me.”

Food claims and ingredient terminology must be checked before the lines become final educational copy.

## Completion

After the last pair:

1. Cards slide together into a circular arrangement.
2. A gold light appears at the centre.
3. Transition to Ondeh-Ondeh's remembering pose.
4. Unlock the final deduction; do not immediately award the card.
