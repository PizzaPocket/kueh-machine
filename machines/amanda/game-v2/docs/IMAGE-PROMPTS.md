# Image-generation prompts

Built-in image generation was used for all assets below. Existing project art was supplied as a visual reference.

## Mobile tray

Recompose the referenced wooden tray held by Beary's two paws into a tall portrait mobile-game frame for a 9:16 phone screen. Match the warm hand-painted storybook style, honey-golden wood, soft brown outlines, subtle grain, and straight-on view. Place the paws at the lower corners, preserve a very large clean central safe area for HTML game content, and include no text, buttons, icons, extra objects, or watermark.

## Ondeh-Ondeh concept sheet

Create a cozy storybook character sheet for an anthropomorphic green ondeh-ondeh coated with delicate coconut flakes. Show a neutral full-body pose plus curious, worried, sudden-realization, and joyful expressions, with a separate golden liquid palm-sugar memory motif. Match Beary's café with soft textured paint, rounded shapes, and light brown outlines. No text, labels, logos, UI, card frame, or watermark; intended for transparent-background dialogue assets.

## Kueh Lapis concept sheet

Create a cozy storybook character sheet for an anthropomorphic rectangular kueh lapis with alternating pink and cream layers. Show a neutral full-body pose plus thoughtful, confused, concentrating, and proud-joy expressions, with separate floating layer pieces. Match Beary's café with soft textured paint, rounded corners, and light brown outlines. No text, labels, logos, UI, card frame, or watermark; intended for transparent-background dialogue assets.

## Ang Ku Kueh concept sheet

Create a cozy storybook character sheet for an anthropomorphic oval red-orange ang ku kueh with a gently embossed tortoise-shell pattern. Show a neutral full-body pose plus shy, puzzled, remembering, and delighted expressions, with separate subtle celebratory motifs and no readable writing. Match Beary's café with soft textured paint, rounded shapes, and light brown outlines. No text, labels, logos, UI, card frame, or watermark; intended for transparent-background dialogue assets.

## Output caveat

The model rendered illustrated backgrounds on the three concept sheets and an opaque outer background on the mobile tray. They are therefore direction/prototyping assets, not final isolated sprites. Generate the final transparent production set only after character approval.

## Ondeh-Ondeh expression prototype

Using the approved Ondeh-Ondeh concept as the identity reference, redraw five consistent full-body poses: neutral-friendly, curious, worried, remembering with a warm golden glow, and joyful. Preserve scale, proportions, pale-green coconut coating, leaves, flower, eyes, cheeks, limbs, textured storybook paint, and brown outlines. Arrange as a separated horizontal sprite sheet with no words or extra objects and request genuine transparency.

## Ondeh-Ondeh Memory Flip prototype

Create a 4-by-3 art sheet of twelve icons forming six conceptual pairs: pandan leaf/fragrance, coconut/coating, palm-sugar block/golden centre, dough/shaped ball, steamer/steam, completed character/memory sparkle. Use warm storybook icon art, clear silhouettes, equal safe areas, no labels or card frames, and request genuine transparency.

## Empty customer-arrival café

Edit the existing café hero into the same wide room without Beary, the held kueh, or the foreground service counter. Reconstruct a clear lower-middle walking band while retaining the window, menu, lamps, display case, shelves, coffee equipment, plants, palette, camera, and storybook style. Add no customer, mascot, dialogue, CTA, or invented text.

## Foreground counter

Create a full-width warm wooden café counter matching the reference room, viewed straight-on and occupying the lower portion of a 16:9 transparent canvas. Keep the middle calm, place only small plant/utensil details near the edges, and include no character, text, food, register, or background. A second background-extraction pass removed the baked checkerboard and produced the final `counter-v2` alpha asset.

## Individual Memory Flip fronts

Six separate square icons were generated from the Ondeh-Ondeh style reference: tied pandan leaves, a wooden bowl of grated coconut, a palm-sugar block with one syrup droplet, a pale-green dough ball with a small unshaped piece, a bamboo steamer with steam, and a golden memory droplet with a central sparkle. Every prompt required one central subject, warm textured storybook paint, rounded forms, brown outlines, mobile readability, no text or card frame, and transparent padding. Because the first outputs printed checkerboards, each icon received a dedicated background-extraction pass; the final files use the `-v2.png` suffix.

## Ondeh-Ondeh individual dialogue sprites

Five separate full-body sprites were generated using the concept sheet and the original neutral crop as identity references: neutral-friendly, curious/thinking, worried with clasped hands, remembering/surprised with small golden rays, and joyful with lifted arms. Every prompt locked the pale-green round body, white coconut shreds, two upper-left leaves, five-petal flower, eye and cheek design, limbs, texture, outline, square canvas, full silhouette, and transparent background. The worried pose required a background-extraction correction; use `worried-v2.png`.

## Ondeh-Ondeh collectible card frame

Create an original 3:4 portrait collectible-character card with a slim blank nameplate, large rounded illustration window in the upper half, blank cream story panel below, and exactly three empty pill-shaped tag frames. Use textured cream paper, caramel wood edges, brown outlines, restrained pandan green and muted rose, and small border-only motifs of leaves, coconut-shred dashes, golden droplets, and sparkles. Do not include a character or text in the bitmap. Avoid all Pokémon-specific borders, symbols, stats, attacks, terminology, typography, rarity marks, and trade dress.
