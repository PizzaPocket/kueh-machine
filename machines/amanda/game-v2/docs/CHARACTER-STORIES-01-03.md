# Character stories 01 to 03

## Purpose

This document contains the fictional emotional narratives for the first three customers. Cultural and historical facts must be researched and verified separately before publication.

Each collectible character card has one short story paragraph. A `Read full story` control reveals the longer narrative without requiring a second version of the card.

## Card story interaction

- Show the short story directly on the character card.
- Add a text button labelled `Read full story` below the short story.
- Open the full story in a themed modal or side panel while leaving the card visible behind it.
- Change the button label to `Close full story` while the panel is open.
- Do not flip the card or create a second card face.
- Preserve the player's collection scroll position when the full story closes.
- The full story must be available after the card is unlocked, not before.
- On mobile, use a full-height sheet with a visible close button and readable scrolling.

## 01. Ondeh-Ondeh

### Chapter title

The Memory Inside

### Arrival

Ondeh-Ondeh arrives looking cheerful but unusually quiet. They remember pandan leaves, grated coconut and the warmth of a steamer, but something important is missing.

They describe feeling hollow, as though everyone expects a sweet surprise from them, but they cannot remember what it is. During the conversation, Beary notices that Ondeh-Ondeh becomes anxious whenever asked what is inside.

### Memory activity

The memory game uncovers images connected to their making: pandan, coconut, dough, steaming and palm sugar. When the final pairs are matched, Ondeh-Ondeh remembers the warm palm-sugar centre hidden inside them.

### Emotional resolution

The restored memory is emotional too. Their sweetness was never meant to be obvious from the outside. It was something protected until they felt safe enough to share it.

### Restored line

> Maybe I wasn't empty after all. I was just keeping something precious inside.

### Short card story

> Wrapped in coconut and coloured by fragrant pandan, Ondeh-Ondeh carries a molten palm-sugar centre. They once mistook their quietness for emptiness, but discovered that some of the sweetest things take time and trust to reveal.

### Full story

Ondeh-Ondeh arrived at Beary's cafe with a smile, but their thoughts felt strangely hollow. They could remember the scent of pandan, the softness of dough and tiny pieces of coconut clinging to their surface. Whenever they tried to remember what belonged at their centre, the memory slipped away.

They worried that everyone was waiting for them to reveal something wonderful. What if there was nothing inside at all?

With Beary's help, scattered memories began returning in pairs. A pandan leaf. A warm steamer. Fresh coconut. A piece of palm sugar. At last, Ondeh-Ondeh remembered the sweetness held safely at their centre.

The memory meant more than an ingredient. Ondeh-Ondeh realised that being quiet did not make them empty. Some feelings are protected until the right moment, and sharing them takes trust.

### Emotional theme

## 02. Kueh Lapis

### Chapter title

One Layer at a Time

### Arrival

Kueh Lapis enters the cafe flustered and apologising for being late. They have been trying to remember everything at once, but each time they hurry, their memories blur together.

They remember warmth, patience and someone carefully watching over them. They also recall being built gradually, but cannot understand why anyone would spend so long making one small cake.

Kueh Lapis worries that none of their individual layers seem important. Each one looks thin and incomplete on its own.

### Memory activity

The player rebuilds Kueh Lapis by arranging thin brown and ivory layers in the correct sequence. Rushing causes the layers to become uneven, so the player must work carefully and steadily.

### Emotional resolution

As the cake becomes whole, Kueh Lapis remembers that every layer was made individually. No single layer carries the entire story. Their strength comes from all the time and care accumulated between them.

### Restored line

> I kept searching for one big memory. But I'm made from all the little ones.

### Short card story

> Kueh Lapis is formed from many delicate layers, each added with patience and care. They once believed their smallest memories did not matter, until they saw how every thin layer helped create something rich, complete and worth waiting for.

### Full story

Kueh Lapis hurried into Beary's cafe, already apologising. They felt certain that an important memory was waiting somewhere, so they had been trying to recover everything at once.

The harder they tried, the more their memories blurred. All they could see were thin brown and ivory lines. Each one seemed too small to contain anything meaningful.

Beary helped Kueh Lapis slow down and rebuild the layers one at a time. Some held warmth. Some held familiar voices. Others remembered quiet moments of waiting while careful hands watched each layer take shape.

When the final layer settled into place, Kueh Lapis understood. They were not missing one enormous memory. They were carrying many small ones, carefully placed together. Progress did not have to happen all at once to matter.

### Tags

- `Singapore`
- `Cake`
- `Layered`

### Minigame

Build the Layers

### Emotional theme

Patience, progress and valuing small steps

## 03. Ang Ku Kueh

### Chapter title

The Shape We Carry

### Arrival

Ang Ku Kueh enters carrying a faint impression on their surface. They know the pattern is important, but cannot remember what it means or why it was carefully pressed into them.

They are friendly but self-conscious. Other kuehs seem soft and naturally shaped, while Ang Ku Kueh feels as though they were expected to carry a symbol before they understood it.

Fragments of memory appear: a carved mould, a banana leaf, red dough and hands pressing a pattern. Ang Ku Kueh worries that without understanding the design, they are only pretending to belong.

### Memory activity

The player reconstructs the missing surface pattern by fitting carved sections into the correct places. Each completed section unlocks a memory connected to family, celebration and good wishes.

### Emotional resolution

Ang Ku Kueh realises that the pattern is not an expectation they must live up to. It is a wish lovingly placed upon them, carrying hope from one generation to another.

### Restored line

> I thought the pattern was telling me who I had to become. It was really someone wishing me a good life.

### Short card story

> Resting on a green leaf and marked with an intricate pattern, Ang Ku Kueh carries wishes of happiness and longevity. They once felt burdened by the symbol on their surface, but learned that it was a gift of hope passed down with care.

### Full story

Ang Ku Kueh entered Beary's cafe with a faint pattern pressed across their surface. They knew it mattered, but they could no longer remember why.

The pattern made them uneasy. It seemed like a message everyone else understood. Ang Ku Kueh wondered whether it contained instructions about who they were supposed to become.

Together, Beary and Ang Ku Kueh recovered fragments of the design. They remembered red dough resting on a green leaf, a carved mould and careful hands pressing every line into place. With each restored section came memories of gatherings, gifts and hopeful wishes shared between generations.

At last, Ang Ku Kueh understood that the pattern was not a demand. It did not decide their future. It was a loving wish placed upon them before their journey began, reminding them that someone had hoped for their happiness and long life.

### Tags

- `SE Asia`
- `Kueh`
- `Moulded`

### Minigame

Restore the Pattern

### Emotional theme

Heritage, expectations and belonging

## Three-chapter emotional progression

1. Ondeh-Ondeh explores what is hidden inside us.
2. Kueh Lapis explores how small experiences build us.
3. Ang Ku Kueh explores what we inherit from others.
