# Beary's Kueh Shop

**By Amanda** — for kuehmachine.com

## Concept

Beary's Kueh Shop is a UX placeholder generator for designers who need charming, ready-to-use filler content instead of generic Lorem Ipsum and stock photos. Its mascot is a bear who works the counter at a kueh café. Opening the tray shows a "What kueh would you like?" picker — Story Time, Cute Icons, Photo Book, or Build your Kueh! — each producing a different kind of placeholder content. It connects to kuehmachine.com directly: a machine that makes kueh, staffed by a bear.

## Look & feel

- **Style:** flat illustration, light brown outlines, warm and tactile rather than slick
- **Palette:** cream background, deep brown outlines/text, soft accent colors (pink, pandan green, butter yellow, purple) borrowed from real kueh; warm orange/terracotta for the tray's tabs and buttons
- **Type:** Baloo 2 for headings (round, friendly), Quicksand for body text
- **Illustrations:** hero, favicon, the tray (wood tray + bear paws), the 4 picker kueh images, the teddy-bear-heart icon, and both Photo Book images are all Amanda's real assets — no more placeholder SVG shapes anywhere in the generator.
