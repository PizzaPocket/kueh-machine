A portfolio-building service for wealth-tier clients

Some things still take time to make.

Real kueh is layered by hand, hours at a time. A good filling needs its time to set, and there's no shortcut for that. The recipes have barely changed in generations, because the slow way is the way that lasts.

We think a portfolio deserves the same patience: no shortcuts, no chasing whatever's trending this quarter, just something assembled deliberately, piece by piece, to hold up over time. Here, each piece is a kueh. What you build from them is a kueh box.
