Classic Kueh Lapis

9 layers · Makes one 7-inch square tray

Ingredients
  • 250g rice flour
  • 125g tapioca starch
  • 500ml full-fat coconut milk
  • 200g caster sugar
  • ½ tsp fine salt
  • Neutral oil, for greasing
Colourings
  • Red — red food colouring (3 layers)
  • Pandan — pandan extract or blended pandan juice (3 layers)
  • Coconut — plain (uncoloured) (3 layers)
Method
  1. Grease a 7-inch square pan and line the base with baking paper.
  2. Whisk rice flour, tapioca starch, coconut milk, sugar, and salt until smooth. Strain through a fine sieve.
  3. Divide batter into 3 equal portions (~167ml each). Stir in colourings: Red → Pandan → Coconut.
  4. Bring steamer to a rolling boil. Preheat the empty pan inside for 2 minutes.
