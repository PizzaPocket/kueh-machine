What is Kueh Machine?

Kueh Machine is the result of a two-month learning and development project run by a design team in Singapore. We explored emerging web development tools by making small projects, meeting for regular check-ins, and sharing feedback as we built. This site brings those experiments together as a collection you can keep exploring, with the Kuehverse connecting them in one shared world.

Kueh (or kuih in Malay) are sweet and savoury snacks that are part of Singapore’s food culture and are found across Southeast Asia. They come in many shapes, colours, and flavours, often using ingredients such as glutinous rice, coconut, gula melaka, and pandan. The nonsense pairing “Kueh Machine” gave everyone a shared starting point without prescribing an outcome. It could be read literally, treated as a meeting of tradition and technology, or taken somewhere else entirely.
